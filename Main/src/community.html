About / Our common ground

A shared rhythm.
A familiar ritual.
A reason to come together.

The Coffee Party brings people together through coffee, music and daytime gatherings. The cup is a conversation starter. The music gives us something to share. The people make it a party.

Come with friends or come on your own. There’s room to find your rhythm, meet someone new and make a little more of your day.

More than a morning out

Come for the coffee.
Stay for the people.

We like the idea that an everyday ritual can open the door to something unexpected: a new conversation, a favourite track, a face you’ll recognise next time.

A good gathering can be the starting point for a bigger idea. Coffee, creativity and collaboration give us plenty of places to begin.
