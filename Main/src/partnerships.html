The Coffee Party is a coffee and music event platform built around bringing people together during the day. For brands, that creates a setting where a product, idea or experience can become part of a shared memory.

We’re interested in partnerships that add something to the gathering: a thoughtful product discovery, a creative installation, a welcoming space or a moment people want to share. A brand activation should feel at home in the room and give guests a reason to engage.

Whether you’re exploring an event sponsorship, a product launch or a bespoke collaboration, we can start with your goals and discuss a format that fits. The conversation covers the audience, setting, creative idea and practical requirements before a scope is agreed.

Partner product
sampling.

A sample is more meaningful when it belongs to the moment. The Coffee Party offers a natural setting to explore product discovery alongside coffee, music and conversation, giving guests a chance to experience something for themselves.

Food, beverage, beauty, lifestyle and wellness brands can get in touch to discuss a sampling idea. Together, we can consider how the product is introduced, where it sits in the guest journey and what a useful interaction looks like.

Before confirming a sampling partnership, we’ll discuss the event, product suitability, quantities, service needs and on-site setup. Tell us what you’d like people to discover; we’ll explore an approach that works for both your brand and the gathering.

Venues

A great venue does more than hold an event. It shapes how people arrive, move, meet and remember the day. We’re open to conversations with cafés, hospitality spaces, cultural venues and distinctive locations that could host a Coffee Party experience.

Venue collaborations can begin with a single gathering or an idea for an ongoing relationship. We’ll discuss the character of your space, capacity, sound requirements, access, service and the guest experience to understand what’s possible.

If you’re looking to introduce your space to a daytime audience or bring a fresh format to an existing community, tell us about it. Share your venue and location when we connect, and we can explore the next steps together.

A few things to know

Partnership
questions, answered.

What is The Coffee Party?+

The Coffee Party brings people together through coffee, music and daytime events. Brand partnerships connect products and ideas with that shared experience.

How can a brand partner with The Coffee Party?+

Brands can start a conversation about event sponsorship, a brand activation, product sampling or a bespoke collaboration. The format, location, creative idea and scope are discussed before anything is confirmed.

Can a brand sample products at a Coffee Party event?+

Product sampling is a partnership opportunity to discuss with the team. Product suitability, event availability, quantities, service and setup requirements are considered for each proposal.

Can venues and artists collaborate with The Coffee Party?+

Yes. Venue operators, DJs, musicians, performers and their representatives can get in touch to explore a collaboration. Venue and artist opportunities depend on creative fit and the needs of each event.

What information is needed to start a partnership conversation?+

Start with your full name, company and email. The initial conversation can cover your goals, preferred timing, location and idea without asking you to complete a long proposal form.
